# OpenScore

**An alternative credit score built on real financial behavior.**

OpenScore is a full-stack fintech platform that reimagines how trust and creditworthiness are measured. Instead of relying on traditional credit history, OpenScore builds a new credit profile using real financial behavior such as rent payments, income stability, and education credentials.

Built at **DeltaHacks 12**, OpenScore aims to make access to credit more fair and inclusive for students, newcomers, freelancers, and gig workers.

---

## 💡 Inspiration

Over **3 million Canadian adults are credit invisible**, with immigrants making up a large portion of this group (Equifax Canada, Statistics Canada).

Many people responsibly pay rent, manage bills, and earn income consistently — yet are denied loans, housing, or education simply because they lack traditional credit history.

We built **OpenScore** to prove that trust can be measured differently.

---

## 🛠️ What OpenScore Does

OpenScore generates an alternative credit score using real financial data and non-traditional indicators.

### How it works:
1. Users securely connect their bank accounts via the **Plaid API**
2. Users upload supporting documents such as:
   - Balance sheets
   - Income statements
   - Education credentials
3. Data is analyzed across **four weighted factors**:

| Factor | Weight | Description |
|------|--------|------------|
| Financial Accounts | 40% | Rent, recurring bills, investments, payment history |
| Alternative Income | 30% | Gig work and non-traditional income sources |
| Cash Flow Volatility | 20% | Income consistency and spending stability |
| Education & Licenses | 10% | Formal education and professional credentials |

These factors are combined into a single **alternative credit score**, accompanied by explanations and simulations.
